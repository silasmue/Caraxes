== 1. KVM probe (kvm-ioeventfd-probe.txt)
  gcc -O2 -Wall -o <scratch>/kvm-ioeventfd-probe caraxes/tools/kvm-ioeventfd-probe.c
  timeout 300 <scratch>/kvm-ioeventfd-probe > kvm-ioeventfd-probe.txt 2>&1
  Result:
  - 4096 datamatch ioeventfds at one address, all registered (NR_IOBUS_DEVS 1000 does not count
    them).
  - EEXIST: the same datamatch twice, a wildcard next to datamatch entries (either order), len 0
    next to len 4. len 8 next to len 4 is accepted. A deassign that matches nothing gets ENOENT.
  - Per write: matched about 1.24 us plus about 25 ns per entry passed in registration order;
    unmatched is the whole scan plus the exit to userspace (4.8 us at N=0, 10.8 us at N=253).
