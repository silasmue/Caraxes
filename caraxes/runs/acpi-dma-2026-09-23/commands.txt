  Why dmacheck and not caraxes_dma_range_test.ko: the test module refuses while a driver is bound, but a map from _DMA exists only
  between dma_configure in really_probe() and device_unbind_cleanup(); with no driver bound the device never has one. dmacheck is a
  PCI driver without id table, bound through driver_override; its probe runs the same checks (256 pages, 8 coherent 64 KiB buffers,
  first/middle/last page of every range, dma_to_phys above the table) and returns -ENODEV. caraxes_dma_range was never loaded in vm3.
