# Windows loop, night of 2026-09-25/26 (branch win-loop-2026-09-26)

Goal: Windows 11 with GeForce 617.14 starts the synthetic RTX 2070 (pure emulation, no host RM).
One boot per iteration (`night-win4.sh`, results in this directory), one model change per iteration.

## Finding of the night: the registry keys switch 617.14 to GSP-RM

- **Keys** (set 22:36 by `regkey-test.sh`, DWORD 1): `EnableGpuFirmware` and `RMEnableGpuFirmware` under
  `HKLM\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Services\nvlddmkm`, its `Parameters` subkey and the class key
  `{4d36e968-e325-11ce-bfc1-08002be10318}\0007`. They take effect at the next boot (a `pnputil /restart-device`
  alone did not switch the path). They stay set: they are the switch.
- **With the keys** the driver skips the CPU-RM falcon chain (no PMU `ENGCTL` poll, no ACR unload) and boots
  GSP-RM exactly as the Linux RM does on this device: FWSEC-FRTS on the GSP falcon (command 0x15 decoded from the
  DMEMMAPPER, WPR2 from guest RAM), Booter Load on SEC2 (WPR meta read, libos args, RMARGS), queues linked (msgq v2),
  then `GMCAPI_GSP_INIT` (system info, registry, static info), `INIT_GSP_TRACE_CRASH_BUFFER` and about 220 RPCs.
- **Evidence**: `regkey-keys-trace.log.gz` (the registry VM: the boot before the keys shows the 60 s PMU poll, the boot after
  the reboot the GSP path), `regkey-keys-rpcs.txt`, `regkey-keys-gpu.log`; X0 repeated it byte for byte in its failing
  controls (`X0-pmu2-stallack200-*`).
- **Where it stops (iteration 0)**: after `GSP_INIT` the kernel RM runs its init (internal controls, a CeUtils channel
  on TURING_DMA_COPY_A, event buffers), then the KMD's adapter-start queries. The device answered 22 controls
  NOT_SUPPORTED, most of them "no host" fallbacks of pure emulation. Right after the last one (`0x20802806`, LPWR,
  not in any public header) the driver frees its clients, sends `GMCAPI_GSP_SUSPEND` (a regular unload), runs Booter
  Unload, and Windows reports Code 43.
- **First divergence from a Linux init**: the Linux RM never sends these adapter-start queries; the first control
  Windows sends that Linux does not is `NV2080_CTRL_CMD_INTERNAL_GET_ENABLED_SEC2_CLASSES` (0x20800aaf), then
  `NV0080_CTRL_CMD_GPU_GET_CLASSLIST_V2` (0x800292); the KMD's own block starts at `0x20809009` (GSS legacy clk).
