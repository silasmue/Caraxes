## Notes
- MemAvailable fell between the runs and this snapshot because of other host work
  (a nix build of about 7 GiB RSS and two cloud-hypervisor processes, `ps` at 14:34).
  No sysmem-iova process was left; the pin counters show acquired - released = 3249,
  the same as before run 1 (host-before.txt: 11745 - 8496).
- The host driver runs with NVreg_ResmanDebugLevel=0 and logs every ioctl. The full
  per-page scans (65,536 queries per 256 MiB, 262,144 per 1 GiB) therefore wrote about
  1.6 million NVRM lines to the kernel log in two minutes (baseline: 6,151 lines in the
  five minutes before), and journald reported three /dev/kmsg overruns. Lines lost in
  those overruns cannot be checked. Among the lines kept, none matches
  assert|fail|error|Xid|IO_PAGE_FAULT. The tool now reads every 2 MiB by default;
  `--full-scan` restores the per-page scan.
- No "Using N-bit DMA addresses" notice for 0000:2d:00.0 this boot: the GPU still
  takes 32-bit IOVAs first.
