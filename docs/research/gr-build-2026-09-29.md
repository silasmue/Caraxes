# Windows GR channel build: the cost is the fence (2026-09-29, C82-lf19)

- **Symptom:** every guest GR channel's host mirror is scheduled 238-397 ms after its first
  doorbell (C82, lf19 97e73264, fence on).
- **Host RM calls of one GR build: about 10 ms** (C82 profile):
  - TSG 0.88 ms, channel 1.35 ms, VAS 1.37 ms.
  - Objects: 0xa140 2.0 ms; 0x902d, 0xc597, 0xc5c0 about 1.0 ms each; 0x5080 0.3 ms;
    0xc5b5 0.25 ms.
  - Schedule 0.43 ms, preemption bind 0.44 ms, FIFO_IDLE 0.49 ms, timeslice 0.33 ms.
  - Warm spare channels would save at most these 10 ms; none are kept.
- **The rest is the new VAS's first plan (260-400 ms):**
  - Windows' D3D device VAS has regions inside the fence that touch set B, so each of their
    leaves gets its own per-leaf object (about 300 per VAS).
  - Each per-leaf object carved the fence alone: one free of the piece it hit plus two
    placeholder allocations around the leaf. C82: object free ~269-296 per plan (113-207 ms);
    fence placeholders ~160 us each, 2 per carve (uncounted).
  - The fence's tail after a carve was one 4 KiB-page placeholder over hundreds of MiB: 0.4-0.7 ms
    per free, 0.16 ms per allocation, at every carve.
  - The same VAS without a fence: 301 per-leaf objects in 15.8 ms.
- **Fix (grbuild1):**
  - A region that gets per-leaf objects carves the fence once for all its leaves in the plan:
    one free per fence piece hit, one placeholder per gap (`fence_carve_many`).
  - A placeholder with an end off a 2 MiB boundary is split there: 4 KiB pages only at the
    unaligned ends, default pages in the middle.
  - A batched leaf the host refuses goes back into the fence: no hole.
  - The guest sees the same VAS: same leaves, same fence coverage; only the host's placeholder
    objects differ.
- **Counters:**
  - Build summary: "GR channel build: 0 from spare, M built at doorbell, time p50/max" (first
    doorbell to commit), other engines apart.
  - Shadow VAS summary at free: "fence: N piece(s) freed (K in batched carves of R region(s)),
    ... split at 2 MiB".
- **Depends on:** F2b (the fence), AB (set-B regions get per-leaf objects), L (shadow layout).
